How fomo radar stays accurate

  • Every post links to its original. Outlets are followed by their verified domain handle, so impostor accounts can't slip in.
  • Claude only sees the live snapshot fomo just fetched, must cite a post or data row for every claim, and tickers that aren't in the data are dropped.
  • Paid DexScreener promotion is labelled and kept separate from organic trending.
  • The bot's scoreboard starts at zero and counts every trade, fees and slippage included.
